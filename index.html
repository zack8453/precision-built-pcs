FAQ

Common questions.

Can you build to a specific budget?

Yes. Add your maximum budget and what games or programs you want to run in the quote form.

Can you upgrade an existing PC?

Yes. Include your current CPU, GPU, RAM and power supply if you know them. If not, describe the PC and what you want improved.

Do I need to choose the parts myself?

No. You can simply explain what you want the PC to do and ask for a recommendation.

Can I request troubleshooting or a repair?

Yes. Describe the symptoms, any error messages, and what happened before the problem started.
